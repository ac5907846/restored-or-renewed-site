Large as the multiplier is, it acts on a small base: most severely damaged houses are restored in place. The upper chart shows what stands at the last roll on the lots of the severely damaged houses of four storms, by the vintage band the house had before the storm, split into the paper’s pathway classes. The lower chart follows the same houses roll by roll for any storm with four or more post-storm rolls. The side card gives the two building-permit records that agree with the roll about who was kept.

Less-documented recovery means only that the roll records no new-construction value on the structure, not that no work was done: where permits can be checked, half of that class pulled repair or roof permits. Condominium units and mobile homes are outside this frame.
