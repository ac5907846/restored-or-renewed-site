Which houses leave follows the comparison an owner makes after the storm: the value left in the structure against the value of the lot. Along that index, exit by the fourth roll falls about a hundredfold in three storms alike, and where the structure left is worth more than the land, exit is at the background rate. The plane splits the index into its two parts among damaged houses, and the side card gives the model estimates and the income check.

Structure value left is the pre-storm improvement value less the value deleted on the first post-storm roll; land value is just value less improvement value. The index orders houses within a storm and does not explain the gap between storms; a roll value near zero is not a missing structure; the pattern is an association, not an estimated structural threshold.
