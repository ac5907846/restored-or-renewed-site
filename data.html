This page records the sources, the damage measure read from consecutive assessment rolls, the pathway classes, the three estimators, and the checks each step had to pass before it entered the paper. Every number is read from the analysis outputs the manuscript uses.

Sources

Damage from the roll pair

For every residential structure with a recorded year built on the pre-storm roll (assessed 1 January of the storm season), the first post-storm roll gives the deleted value, the new just value, and whether the structure is still present. A severe loss is a structure removed from the roll, a deleted value of half or more of the pre-storm improvement value (just value less land value), or a just value below half its pre-storm level. Rolls before 2010 carry no deleted value, so the 2004 and 2005 storms use just value and removal alone. The same classes on the roll pair before the storm season give the county’s routine rate; the storm-pair rate minus that is the excess. A county enters the damage set with an excess of at least 1 per 100 structures or at least .25 inspector-recorded destroyed dwellings per 100 parcels.

Following the parcel

Parcels are followed across rolls by the parcel identifier with punctuation removed, and where that fails by the Department’s statewide parcel identifier, which survived Lee County’s renumbering of Lehigh Acres between the 2022 and 2023 rolls.

Pathways and estimators

Each severely damaged site-built house on its own lot is followed up to eight post-storm rolls into one pathway: documented repair (the roll records new-construction value on the pre-storm structure), less-documented recovery (the pre-storm structure stands with no such value recorded), replaced (a structure with a post-storm year built), or cleared (demolished or absent from the last roll). Exit is replaced or cleared. The renewal window is a discrete-time hazard with no model. The redevelopment threshold is a linear probability model of exit by roll 4 on the log of structure value left over land value, with county-storm fixed effects, controls for pre-1994 vintage, log pre-storm just value and homestead exemption, and tract-clustered errors. The next-storm exposure sets each pathway against the intact old stock of the same tracts with tract fixed effects or matched strata, over four samples and with each county left out in turn.

Outside checks

Two records kept independently of the roll: FEMA inspections, against which the damage measure and the exit rates are compared, and municipal building permits, against which the pathway classes are compared.

Lineage

The analysis is a chain of numbered stages, one folder per stage, each writing tabular results that the next stage and the figure scripts read. One build step rewrites this site’s data layer from those results and prints the published values beside the ones the paper quotes, so the pages and the manuscript cannot disagree. The site shows aggregates, estimates and intervals; no individual property record is published.
