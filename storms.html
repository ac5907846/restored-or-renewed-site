The roll pair around each storm records damage on the parcel: on the first post-storm roll a structure is a severe loss when it is removed, when the value the county appraiser deleted is half or more of its pre-storm improvement value, or when its just value has fallen below half. The map tints each county by its excess severe-loss rate, the storm pair’s rate over the county’s routine rate in the roll pair before the season. The tour walks through the sixteen storms in order of landfall; pick one to hold it, and hover a county for the numbers behind the shade.
